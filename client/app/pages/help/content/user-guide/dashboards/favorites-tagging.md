The same favorites and tagging mechanics that work for queries (see [Favorites & Tagging](/help/user-guide/querying/favorites-tagging)) also apply to dashboards.

## Favorites

Click the star icon next to any dashboard title to favorite it. The star turns yellow to confirm. Favorites appear:

-   In the home page _Favorites_ panel.
-   In the navbar dropdown menus.
-   As a filter on the dashboard list view.

![Favorite a dashboard](/content/help/assets/dashboard-favorites/dashboard-favorites-00-favorite.gif)

## Tagging

Hover over a dashboard title and the _+ Add Tag_ button appears. The tag dialog suggests previously-used tags as you type, so you can keep a flat, predictable taxonomy (`finance`, `risk`, `production`, `experimental`…).

![Add or edit tags](/content/help/assets/dashboard-favorites/dashboard-favorites-01-edit_tags.gif)

Tags appear in the right-hand rail of the dashboard list. Click a tag to filter; click again to clear. Shift-click to combine tags.
