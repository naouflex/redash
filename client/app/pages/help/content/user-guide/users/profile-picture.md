If you sign in to Rewatch with a Google account, your Google avatar is fetched automatically. Otherwise, the platform falls back to [Gravatar](https://en.gravatar.com/) keyed on your sign-in email address.

If you don't already have a Gravatar, sign up with the email address you use to sign in. Avatars are cached by the browser, so it can take up to about 5 minutes (and a page refresh) for a new avatar to appear after you set or update it.
