As your collection of queries and dashboards grows from a few hundred to a few thousand, favorites and tags are how you keep things findable.

![Favorites and tagging](/content/help/assets/favorites-tagging/favorites-tagging-00-favorite-tag.gif)

## Favorites {#Favorites}

Click the star icon next to any dashboard or query title to favorite it. The star turns yellow to confirm. Favorites appear in three places:

-   The home page, as a "Favorites" panel.
-   The navbar dropdowns for Queries and Dashboards.
-   As a filter on the query and dashboard list pages.

![Favorites](/content/help/assets/favorites-tagging/favorites-tagging-01-favorites.gif)

## Tagging

You can tag both queries and dashboards. Hover over the title and click the _+ Add Tag_ button that appears. The dialog suggests previously-used tags as you type. Pick as many as you need and save. Press `Esc` to abort.

![Add tag](/content/help/assets/favorites-tagging/favorites-tagging-02-tagging-example.png)

A small, predictable tag vocabulary makes a big difference to onboarding new teammates. Agree on a flat set of common tags up front (`finance`, `risk`, `frontier`, `firm`, `experimental`…) before letting tags grow organically.

On the list pages, tags appear in the right-hand rail. Click a tag to filter the list; click again to clear. Shift-click to combine tags.

![Filtering by tag](/content/help/assets/favorites-tagging/favorites-tagging-03-tagging.gif)
