The Funnel visualization is the simplest in the catalogue: it needs exactly two fields from your query, `step` and `value`. Rewatch ranks the steps by value and renders the trapezoid funnel automatically.

![Funnel visualization example](/content/help/assets/funnel-viz/funnel-viz-00-funnel.gif)

It's particularly useful for product funnels (visit → sign-up → activation → paid) and on-chain flows (visit → connect wallet → sign permit → execute).
