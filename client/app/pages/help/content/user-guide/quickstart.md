The fastest way to feel productive inside Rewatch is to ship one small dashboard end to end. This page walks you through the three steps every analyst takes on day one.

## 1. Write a query

Click _Create_ in the navbar and pick _Query_. Choose a data source, write some SQL (or YAML for HTTP / EVM / Dune sources), and hit _Execute_.

![Writing a query](/content/help/assets/quickstart/quickstart-00-writing_a_query.gif)

If you need a deeper tour of the editor, schema browser and shortcuts, jump to [Creating and editing queries](/help/user-guide/querying/writing-queries).

## 2. Add a visualization

By default your results render as a plain table. To turn that into a chart, click _New Visualization_ above the results pane and pick the type that best fits your data. Rewatch supports a long list of chart types out of the box (line, bar, area, pie, scatter, funnel, sankey, choropleth maps and more), each with sensible defaults.

![Adding a visualization](/content/help/assets/quickstart/quickstart-01-add_visualization.gif)

For the full catalogue see [Visualization Types](/help/user-guide/visualizations/visualizations-types) and the [Visualizations how-to](/help/user-guide/visualizations/visualizations-how-to).

## 3. Build a dashboard

Click _Create_ in the navbar again, pick _Dashboard_, give it a name, then drop in your visualization with _Add Widget_. You can mix in text boxes for context, group widgets together, and share or embed the result.

![Creating a dashboard](/content/help/assets/quickstart/quickstart-02-create_dashboard.gif)

For dashboard refresh schedules, filters and permissions, see [Creating and editing dashboards](/help/user-guide/dashboards) and [Sharing and embedding dashboards](/help/user-guide/dashboards/sharing-dashboards).

That's it: query → visualization → dashboard. From here, browse the sidebar to dive into [Alerts](/help/user-guide/alerts), [Machine Learning](/help/user-guide/machine-learning) or any of the data sources you'd like to plug in.
